# AutoHeal DevOps — Controlled Pilot Readiness & Operational Safety Report

## 1. Executive Summary

AutoHeal DevOps SaaS Phase 10 establishes the operational readiness framework for customer **Controlled Pilot Deployments**.

The controlled pilot framework allows enterprise DevOps and SRE teams to evaluate autonomous incident detection, AI diagnosis, and remediation workflows within live environments **without risking unintended production service disruption**.

---

## 2. Mandatory Pilot Guardrails & Safety Locks

### 2.1 Enforced Simulation Mode (`RECOVERY_SIMULATION_MODE=true`)
- In controlled pilot deployments, `RECOVERY_SIMULATION_MODE` is locked to `True` by default.
- Remediation actions (pod restarts, traffic shedding, scaling) are executed in a simulated dry-run mode:
  - Pre-flight checks are evaluated.
  - Action envelopes are signed and transmitted over mTLS to the agent.
  - The agent logs the exact command and target parameters without mutating live system state.
  - Health checks and post-verification logic validate the simulated outcome.

### 2.2 Operator-in-the-Loop Human Approval
- Full autonomy is prohibited during initial pilot phases.
- Every recovery plan generated by the AI Diagnosis Engine requires explicit approval by an operator possessing the `engineer` or `admin` role.
- Approval timeouts (default: 15 minutes) automatically discard unapproved actions to prevent stale state execution.

### 2.3 Instant Safety Abort & Circuit Breaker
- An emergency "Kill Switch" endpoint (`POST /api/v1/incidents/kill-switch`) immediately halts all active and pending remediation workflows.
- Circuit breakers automatically trip if more than 3 remediation failures occur within a 10-minute window, locking the service into read-only observation mode.

## 4. Pilot Success Criteria & KPIs

Enterprise pilot engagements measure success against three primary metrics over a 30-day evaluation window:

1. **Mean Time to Remediate (MTTR) Reduction**: Target >= 60% reduction in MTTR for common recurring incidents (e.g., OOM kills, stuck worker processes, connection pool saturation).
2. **Alert Noise Suppression**: Target >= 45% reduction in paging alerts through intelligent incident grouping and duplicate alert suppression.
3. **Diagnostic Accuracy**: Target >= 85% operator acceptance rate of AI-generated root cause analyses and recovery action plans.
